# Launch Copy — Reusable Short Copy

## 5 taglines
1. Swipe. Match. Swap.
2. Same ukay energy, faster match.
3. Trade what you don't need for what you do.
4. No buyer, no seller — just a match.
5. Every swap counts. Literally.

## App-store-style short description (under 170 characters)
Swipe to trade what you don't need for what you do. Mutual match opens the chat, you arrange the handoff. Barter-first, with a Circular Passport that tracks your impact.

Longer version (if a store listing allows more):
> PeekPick is a swipe-based barter app. List what you don't need, swipe on what you want, and when it's mutual on both sides, the chat opens — no more messaging into silence. Barter is the point; cash top-ups are optional. Meet up or ship it yourself (LBC, J&T, wherever works). Every completed trade builds your Circular Passport — items reused, waste diverted, CO2 saved — a record of what you've kept out of the landfill.

## The one-liner (when someone asks "what do you do?")
"I built an app where you swipe on stuff instead of people — you trade what you don't need for what you do, and it only matches you when the other person wants your item too."

Shorter backup version:
"It's Tinder, but for swapping stuff instead of dating."

## 30-second verbal pitch
"So you know how sa Facebook groups, you post 'swap for X,' and either nobody replies or somebody says 'interested' and disappears? I built PeekPick to fix that. You list stuff you don't need — clothes, books, gadgets, whatever — and you swipe through what other people are offering. When you both swipe on each other's item, that's a match, and only then does the chat open. So you're never chasing someone who was never actually interested. It's barter-first — item for item, you can add a bit of cash on top if you both want, but that's optional. You still meet up or ship it yourselves, same as you'd do now. And every trade you finish adds to your Circular Passport — a record of how many items you've reused and how much waste you've kept out of the landfill. We're just getting started here in Iloilo, so anyone who joins now is basically a founding member."

## How to use this
Use the taglines as post captions, poster headlines, or bio lines — pick whichever fits the space, don't force all five into one place. The one-liner is for casual run-ins (jeepney, canteen, org fair booth) when someone asks what you're building — memorize the short backup version first, it's easier to say naturally. The 30-second pitch is for when someone's actually curious and has a minute — practice it out loud a few times so it doesn't sound read off a script.
