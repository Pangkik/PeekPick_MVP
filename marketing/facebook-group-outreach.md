# Facebook Group Outreach — Iloilo Swap/Ukay/Buy-Sell Groups

This is the most important file. The goal is to get invited in, not to sneak in. Admins ban people who show up and post links cold. They welcome people who ask first and offer something to the members, not to themselves.

---

## (a) DM to a group admin — asking permission to post

Send this as a Facebook Messenger DM to the group admin/moderator, not a comment on a post. Personalize the bracketed parts.

> Hi [Admin name], kumusta! I'm Rain, taga-Iloilo din. I run a small barter app called PeekPick — swipe-based swap app, parang Tinder but for trading stuff instead of matching people.
>
> Sobrang bagay siya sa group niyo kasi ganito talaga ang ginagawa ng mga members — nag-a-ukay, nagpapalit ng items. Gusto ko lang sana i-try sa members niyo kung makakatulong siya para mas mabilis mag-match ang mga gusto mag-swap, instead na mag-scroll through hundreds of posts.
>
> Puwede ko po ba i-share sa group once lang, or kung ok lang sa inyo, paminsan-minsan lang, walang spam, promise. Kung may members kayo na willing mag-try, masaya rin ako mag-follow up personally para sila mismo mag-guide sa app (feedback pa rin kasi kami, bagong-bago lang).
>
> Whatever po ang tingin niyong tama for your group, sundan ko lang. Salamat sa oras niyo!

Why this works: it names the admin, states who Rain is plainly, frames the ask as "may I" not "I'm going to," and offers to follow the admin's rules rather than dictate terms. It also admits the app is new — that's honest, and admins trust honesty over hype.

## (b) Group post — once permission is granted

Post this once approved. Keep it conversational, not a press release. Adjust group name/emoji use to match the group's usual tone (check 3-4 recent posts first).

> Hoy mga taga-swap dito sa group! 👋
>
> Sino pa rin nakakaranas nito: nag-post ka ng item mo, "swap for [X]," tapos wala man lang comment. O may nag-comment na "interested" tapos gone na pagka-message mo. Ganon din ako eh, kaya may ginawa akong app.
>
> PeekPick — swipe ka lang sa mga item ng iba, at kung parehas kayong gusto ng isa't isa (like, gusto mo ng sapatos niya, gusto niya naman ng jacket mo), doon lang mag-o-open ang chat niyo. Wala nang paghintay kung sino sasagot. Match talaga, dalawang panig.
>
> Barter pa rin ang laman — item for item, pwede ring lagyan ng cash top-up kung gusto niyo pareho, pero hindi kailangan. Meet-up or courier (LBC/J&T), kayo pa rin bahala kung paano ihatid, same as dati.
>
> May email verification, may report/block, may rating pagkatapos mag-trade — safety features na wala sa ibang platform. At bonus: may Circular Passport siya na nagre-record kung ilang items na na-reuse mo, ilang kilong basura na-divert, at CO2 na-save — parang track record mo bilang taga-swap, keeper mo yun.
>
> Bago pa lang siya dito sa Iloilo (founding members pa lang kami!) kaya kung susubukan niyo, sasalo ako personally kung may tanong or naguluhan kayo sa app. Link: https://peek-pick-final.vercel.app
>
> Try niyo lang, libre naman, at swap culture din naman talaga ito, tinulungan lang natin siyang mas mabilis mag-jodo. 😄

Notes:
- "founding members" framing turns "no users yet" into an invite, not a weakness.
- Do not claim it's "used by hundreds of Ilonggos" or anything similar — it isn't yet.
- Swap in local phrasing the specific group actually uses (some say "palit," some say "swap," some mix English) — match what you already see in that group's posts.

## (c) Comment-reply script — 5 likely objections

**"Is this a scam?"**
> Hindi po, honest answer: bago lang talaga siya, kaya nga sinasabi kong founding members pa lang tayo dito. Live na yung app, pwede niyo i-try mismo (link sa post), may email verification bago ka makapag-swipe, at hindi kami humihingi ng payment or bank details kahit kailan. Kung may mangyari na hindi maganda sa trade, may report/block feature din po para ma-flag agad.

**"Bakit hindi na lang Marketplace?"**
> Pwede pa rin naman ang Marketplace, ito lang extra option kung gusto mo mas mabilis mag-match specifically para sa swap/barter (hindi bili-bili). Sa Marketplace kasi minsan ikaw pa maghahanap ng buyer or ikaw mag-scroll ng ilang oras. Dito, mag-swipe ka lang sa gusto mo, at kung match kayo ng may-ari, doon lang bubukas ang chat — di na kailangan mag-DM ng random tapos hindi na sasagot.

**"May bayad ba ito?"**
> Wala pong bayad. Barter talaga ang core — item for item. Kung gusto niyo maglagay ng konting cash sa offer (halimbawa may sobrang halaga yung item ng isa), option lang yun, hindi requirement.

**"Paano kung hindi magpakita yung isang tao?"**
> Same rules pa rin as dati sa swap groups — meet sa public place (mall, terminal, campus), or gamitin ang courier (LBC/J&T) kung magkalayo kayo. Dun sa app, may rating system pagkatapos ng trade, kaya kung may mag-no-show or manloko, may record yun at makikita ng iba pag nag-check sila ng profile niya bago mag-agree ng trade.

**"Safe ba ilagay yung items ko dito?"**
> Same principle lang po as pag nagpo-post ka sa group — huwag maglagay ng sensitive info sa item photos (address, ID, etc). Sa app side, may email verification bago makapag-transact ang isang account, may report/block kung may kaduda-duda, at hindi namin kailangan ng bank or payment info dahil barter ang core ng app.

## (d) DON'Ts — how to get banned from a group

1. **Don't post before an admin says yes.** Cold-posting a link in a group you just joined is the #1 reason posts get deleted and members get banned/muted.
2. **Don't post the same copy-paste across 10 groups back to back.** Admins compare notes, especially in overlapping Iloilo groups. Post in one, wait, adjust tone per group.
3. **Don't drop just a link with no context.** A bare link reads as spam/ad even with permission — always lead with the member's problem, put the link at the end.
4. **Don't argue with objections in the comments.** Answer once, calmly, factually (use the scripts above), then let it go. Getting defensive in a comment thread is what gets a post reported.
5. **Don't claim user numbers, reviews, or "trusted by" anything that isn't true.** No users yet is fine to say. A fabricated stat, if caught out, kills trust in the group permanently — and Iloilo Facebook circles are small, word travels.
6. **Don't tag the admin publicly asking them to boost your post.** Ask privately via DM if you want a pin or share; public tagging feels like pressure.
7. **Don't post more than once every couple weeks in the same group** unless the admin explicitly says post more often. Repetition reads as spam even if each post is different.
8. **Don't use referral/incentive language** ("invite 3 friends and get...") in these early posts — there's no such program, and groups are especially sensitive to anything that smells like MLM.

## How to use this
Start with 2-3 groups you're already a member of or know the admin of personally — warmest relationships first. Send the DM in (a), wait for a yes, then post (b) adapted to that group's tone. Keep the comment scripts in (c) open in a note on your phone so you can answer fast without overthinking each reply. Re-read the DON'Ts before every new group you approach — this list is your guardrail against getting banned and losing that group forever.
