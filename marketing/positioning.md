# PeekPick — Positioning

## One-sentence positioning
PeekPick is a swipe-to-swap app where you trade stuff you don't need for stuff you do, and every trade counts toward a Circular Passport that proves you're actually doing something good for the planet and your barangay.

## The core promise
You already know how to do this — ukay-ukay, group chats, "swap tayo," barkada trading clothes and books before finals. PeekPick just makes the matching part faster and safer: swipe on what you want, get matched only when it's mutual, chat inside the app, and walk away with a running record (items reused, kg of CO₂ saved, kg of waste diverted) that's yours to keep and show.

## 3 proof points
1. **Mutual match, not cold DM.** You only start chatting when both sides want each other's item — no more "is this still available" into silence, no negotiating with someone who was never going to trade.
2. **Built-in safety tools.** Email verification, daily swipe limits, report/block, and ratings after each trade — the basics Facebook Marketplace doesn't give you.
3. **The Circular Passport.** Every completed trade adds to a personal, portable record of what you reused and what you kept out of the landfill — badges included. ## Why now / why here
Iloilo already runs on swap culture — ukay finds, barter groups, dorm-mates trading uniforms and textbooks every semester. What's missing isn't the behavior, it's the matching. Facebook groups make you scroll hundreds of posts and DM five people before one replies. PeekPick is that same behavior with a swipe instead of a scroll, and a mutual-match instead of a maybe. It's launching first with students and young professionals in Iloilo because this is where the habit is strongest and the trust circles (same school, same barangay, same org) are tightest — the exact conditions barter needs to feel safe.

## Who this is NOT for
- People who want to buy and sell for cash as a primary hustle — PeekPick is barter-first; cash is only an optional sweetener on top of an item-for-item trade, not the point.
- Anyone expecting overnight, citywide inventory — this is a founding cohort in Iloilo. If someone wants instant scale (thousands of listings today), Facebook Marketplace already has that.
- People who won't do their own meet-up or courier arrangement — PeekPick matches and lets you chat; you still coordinate handoff yourselves (meet-up, LBC, J&T), same as any swap you'd do now.
- Businesses/resellers doing high-volume flipping — this is peer-to-peer, personal-closet-and-shelf stuff, not a storefront.

## Message tests
Try these as standalone lines (Facebook comments, poster headlines, DM openers) and note which gets replies, saves, or shares:

1. "Swipe. Match. Swap. No more 'is this still available?' into the void."
2. "Same ukay energy, mas mabilis lang mag-match." (Same ukay energy, just faster matching.)
3. "Trade your old stuff, keep the receipts — literally. Every swap builds your Circular Passport."
4. "You already swap with your barkada. PeekPick just finds you a match outside it."
5. "No buyer, no seller — just two people who both want what the other has."

## How to use this
Pick one or two message tests per week and drop them as-is into a Facebook post, comment, or poster caption. Watch which ones get replies or shares — that tells you which angle to lean into for the next batch of copy. Don't run all five at once; you won't know which one worked.
