# 4-Week Launch Content Calendar

Built for one solo, non-technical, zero-budget founder with about 20 minutes a day to give this. The plan assumes Facebook is the only platform in active use; Instagram is optional and only happens if a week has slack.

**The rule that matters more than any of this:** consistency beats volume. Three posts a week, every week, replied to promptly, will outperform a burst of ten posts followed by two weeks of silence. If a week goes sideways, cut posting before you cut replying to comments — the people already talking to you matter more than the next new post.

---

## Daily 20-minute budget (on posting days)

- 5 min — copy/paste the day's post from `launch-posts.md`, attach the photo/screenshot, publish.
- 10 min — reply to every comment on today's post and yesterday's post. Thank people by name, answer questions directly, and if someone shows real interest, invite them to try the app right there in the reply.
- 5 min — check the app for any new signups or messages; if someone joined, send them a short personal welcome message.

On non-posting days, the 20 minutes is just: check comments/DMs from the last 48 hours, reply, done.

## Handling comments, every week

- Reply to everyone, even a "nice!" — a short reply costs 10 seconds and this is the whole audience right now.
- If someone asks a real question (how does shipping work, is it free, what if the other person doesn't show up), answer honestly in the comment, in public — other lurkers have the same question.
- If someone signs up because of a comment thread, say so later ("welcome to the first batch of swappers!") — it rewards the behavior publicly without needing fake stats.
- Never delete a skeptical or critical comment unless it's abusive or spam. Answer it instead — a founder engaging honestly with doubt is more convincing than silence.

---

## What to cut on a bad week

In order, cut in this sequence before cutting comment replies:

1. Drop from 3 posts to 1 post for the week — repost or lightly refresh whichever post from `launch-posts.md` performed best previously instead of writing something new.
2. Skip Instagram entirely (it was always optional).
3. Skip the "behind the scenes" extras — stick to the core 3-post skeleton only when it's back to normal.
4. Never skip: replying to comments and DMs from the past 48 hours. That's the floor. If only 5 minutes exist in a day, spend them there, not on a new post.

---

## How to use this

- This is a plan, not a script — swap days around freely as long as the weekly rhythm (roughly 3 posts/week, replies every day) holds.
- Don't try to "catch up" by dumping several unposted entries in one day if a week gets missed — just resume the schedule from wherever you are. A gap is fine; a flood looks like spam.
- Re-check in week 5 whether 3 posts/week is still sustainable, and either hold steady or scale back to 2/week rather than burning out and going silent.
