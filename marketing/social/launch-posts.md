# Launch Posts — Facebook (primary platform)

12 ready-to-post pieces. Each has: the post text, what visual to pair it with, and the one job it's doing. Mix of English and Taglish — Taglish is kept clean rather than forcing Hiligaynon phrasing that could read as machine-translated.

---

### 1. The problem people recognize (Taglish)

> May closet o drawer ka bang puno ng gamit na hindi mo na ginagamit, pero ayaw mo pa ring itapon?
>
> Baka may ibang tao dito sa Iloilo na kailangan talaga niyan — at may hawak sila na kailangan mo naman.
>
> That's literally the whole idea behind PeekPick.

**Visual:** Photo of a cluttered closet, drawer, or "junk shelf" — relatable, slightly messy, not stock-photo perfect.
**Job:** Get the reader nodding before they even know what PeekPick is.

---

### 2. How it works, in one breath (English)

> Here's PeekPick in one breath: you list what you own, you swipe on what other people own, and when you both like each other's stuff, you get a chat. You arrange the swap yourselves.
>
> No selling, no listing fees, no shipping surprises. Just people trading things they actually have for things they actually need.

**Visual:** Simple 3-step graphic — List / Swipe & Match / Chat & Swap. Screenshots from the actual app, not illustrations.
**Job:** Remove confusion. This is the post that answers "wait, what is this exactly?"

---

### 3. Introducing the Circular Passport (English/Taglish)

> Every swap on PeekPick updates something called your Circular Passport — a running tally of how many items you've kept out of the trash, how much CO2 you've saved, and badges for the kind of swapper you are.
>
> Parang proof, in actual numbers, na hindi lang basta decluttering ang ginagawa mo — may kontribusyon ka rin sa Iloilo.

**Visual:** Mockup/screenshot of the Passport screen from the app (items reused count, CO2 saved, badges).
**Job:** Introduce the differentiator feature and make it feel worth showing off later.

---

### 4. Founding-member invite (Taglish)

> PeekPick just launched and may zero users pa. Wala pang isa.
>
> Gusto ko na ikaw ang una. Kung sasali ka ngayon, ikaw mismo ang bubuo kung ano ang maging itong app — sasabihin mo sa akin kung ano ang sira, ano ang nakakalito, ano ang gusto mong idagdag.
>
> First people who join get to shape this from scratch. Comment "SWAP" or message me and I'll walk you through it personally.

**Visual:** Plain text graphic — "Founding Swappers Wanted." No stock photos, keep it low-key and personal.
**Job:** Recruit first real users by offering ownership, not by faking urgency.

### 5. Honest "we're brand new, help us build this" (English)

> Full transparency: PeekPick is brand new. I built it alone. I'm not a developer by background, and there's no team, no funding, no marketing budget behind this post — just me trying to solve something I kept seeing around Iloilo.
>
> That also means the app isn't perfect yet. If something breaks, tell me. If something's confusing, tell me. I'd rather fix it fast than pretend it's flawless.

**Visual:** Behind-the-scenes photo — Rain's actual laptop/phone screen with the app open, not a polished product shot.
**Job:** Build trust through radical honesty; pre-empt the "is this legit?" skepticism.

### 6. Ukay-ukay and bayanihan framing (Taglish)

> Ukay-ukay culture is already proof na open tayo sa second-hand. Bayanihan naman nagturo sa atin na mas okay mag-tulungan kaysa mag-isa.
>
> Sinusundan lang ni PeekPick ang dalawang bagay na 'yan, tapos ginawa siyang app. Same spirit — mas madali lang hanapin yung tugma.

**Visual:** Photo of an ukay-ukay rack, or a simple illustration representing bayanihan (community carrying something together).
**Job:** Root the product in values the audience already holds, so it doesn't feel like a foreign concept.

### 7. Student-budget angle (Taglish)

> Estudyante ka ba na sawa na sa presyo ng bago? PeekPick is for you.
>
> Yung damit na hindi mo na kasya, yung mga libro na tapos ka na, yung gadget na di mo na ginagamit pero okay pa — pwede mo palitan sa kung ano ang kulang mo. Walang pera na kailangan gastusin, unless gusto mo talaga.

**Visual:** Dorm room or student desk photo — books, clothes, a charger, everyday clutter.
**Job:** Speak directly to the broke-student pain point and frame barter as the practical answer.

### 8. Safety and trust reassurance (English)

> Before you swap with a stranger online, a fair question: is this safe?
>
> On PeekPick, you don't share your address or number until you decide to. Every match happens inside a chat first, and there's a report and block feature if someone's being weird. It's still on you to meet in public places and trust your gut — the app just gives you the tools to check someone out before you commit to anything.

**Visual:** Screenshot of the chat screen or the report/block feature in the app.
**Job:** Address the trust objection directly, without overpromising safety the app can't guarantee.

### 9. Circular Passport as a shareable milestone (Taglish)

> Isipin mo, tapos ang isang swap, tapos may lalabas na parang report card — pero sa halip na grades, ilang gamit na ang na-reuse mo at ilang kilo ng CO2 ang na-save mo.
>
> 'Yun ang Circular Passport sa PeekPick. Proof na, oo, may ginagawa ka talagang maganda, hindi lang basta paglilinis ng bahay.

**Visual:** Sample Passport card mockup, styled like something worth screenshotting.
**Job:** Reinforce the Passport with a more personal, "you'll want to share this" framing.

### 10. The problem, reframed (English)

> A shirt you wore twice. A phone charger you don't need anymore because you switched brands. A textbook from a class you finished last semester.
>
> None of that is trash. It's just sitting in the wrong house. PeekPick exists to get it to the right one.

**Visual:** Split image — "sitting in a drawer" vs. "being used by someone else." Simple, no text overlay needed.
**Job:** Emotional hook that reframes clutter as latent value — a shareable, quotable post.

### 11. Direct call-to-action (English)

> PeekPick is live at peek-pick-final.vercel.app. No app store, no download — just open it in your browser, make an account, list one thing you own, and start swiping.
>
> It costs nothing to try, it takes about five minutes, and honestly, being one of the first people on it in Iloilo is kind of the whole appeal right now.

**Visual:** Simple graphic with the URL and/or a QR code linking to the app.
**Job:** Give the lowest-friction possible next step, right when someone's ready to act.

### 12. Bayanihan close-out (Taglish)

> Wala pa kaming users, pero may direksyon kami — gawin itong lugar kung saan mas madali mag-bigay at mag-tanggap ng gamit dito sa Iloilo, without always needing pera.
>
> Kung naniniwala ka rin diyan, sumali ka. Hindi ito app lang na gusto naming palakihin. Gusto naming ito'y kilalanin bilang bagay na ginawa ng Iloilo, para sa Iloilo.

**Visual:** Group photo (real, if available) or a simple illustration of community/hands passing an item along.
**Job:** Close the launch series on identity and belonging — the "why join" answer for anyone still on the fence.

---

## How to use this

- Don't post all 12 in a row. Spread them across the 4-week calendar (see `content-calendar.md`) — roughly 3 per week.
- Post from Rain's personal profile and/or the PeekPick Facebook page, and also drop the relevant ones into Iloilo-local Facebook groups where self-promotion is allowed (check each group's rules first).
- Swap in a real photo/screenshot before posting — none of these should go out with a stock image or no image at all; native-feeling visuals get more reach than link previews.
- If Taglish phrasing ever sounds off to an actual Ilonggo ear, switch that line to English rather than force it — a clean sentence beats an awkward one.
